🧠 What is a Vector Database?

Normal database:

id | name
1  | John
2  | Alice



Stores structured data.
Vector database:
id | embedding
1  | [0.21, -0.77, 0.93, ...]
2  | [-0.11, 0.54, -0.22, ...]
Stores vectors (embeddings).
