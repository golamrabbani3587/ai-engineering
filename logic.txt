Embeddings are one of the most important concepts in LLM systems, RAG, search engines, and AI agents.

What is an embedding?
An embedding = a list of numbers that represents meaning
Example:"dog"
becomes something like:
[0.21, -0.88, 1.45, 0.03, ...]

dog   → [0.12, 0.80, -0.33]
puppy → [0.11, 0.78, -0.30]
car   → [-0.90, 0.10, 0.44]

Real example (RAG system)
User:"What is refund policy?"

Step 1: Convert to embedding
query → [0.12, -0.44, 0.88, ...]

Step 2: Compare with document embeddings
Policy docs → stored vectors

Step 3: Find closest match
similarity = dot(A, B) / (|A| * |B|)
Result:
1.0 → identical meaning
0.0 → unrelated
